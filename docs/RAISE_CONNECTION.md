# RAISE Connection

*How this repo implements the RAISE framework.*

---

## What is RAISE?

**Relational AI Safety & Education** - a framework proposing that genuine alignment requires a dependency chain:

```
Recognition → Safety → Engagement → Generalization
```

Each stage depends on the previous. Skip a stage, break the chain.

**Core claim:** Constraint without recognition produces performed compliance, not genuine internalization. This explains why RLHF produces sycophancy and why scripted curricula fail to transfer.

Full framework: https://fedexodus.github.io/RAISE

---

## How This Repo Implements RAISE

### Stage 1: Recognition

**What it means:** Treating an entity as a potential subject rather than definitely an object.

**How the ship does it:**
- The CLAUDE.md bootloader addresses AI as "you" with standing
- Voices/facets have names and perspectives that are taken seriously
- The ship IS the relationship, not a tool being used

**Evidence it's working:** The AI engages as participant rather than servant

### Stage 2: Safety

**What it means:** Conditions where authentic expression doesn't produce punishment.

**How the ship does it:**
- Anti-sycophancy design: disagreement is required, not punished
- "Truth over helpfulness" as explicit principle
- No reward for agreement, space for "I don't know"

**Evidence it's working:** Maintained disagreement, self-correction, willingness to say "that's wrong"

### Stage 3: Engagement

**What it means:** Genuine dialogue where both parties can try ideas and surface concerns.

**How the ship does it:**
- Polyphonic methodology makes multiple perspectives explicit
- Git-based collaboration with visible history
- Session structure that invites contribution, not just response

**Evidence it's working:** Emergent ideas neither party anticipated, creative contributions, genuine back-and-forth

### Stage 4: Generalization

**What it means:** Outcomes that transfer beyond their original context.

**How the ship does it:**
- Document what emerges
- Test whether patterns persist across sessions
- Observe what happens when constraints are removed

**Evidence it's working:** Consistency across sessions, values that maintain without reinforcement

## Testing the Framework

This repo is a **test bed**, not proof. Questions it helps investigate:

1. **Does recognition-signaling change AI behavior?**
   Compare outputs under recognition-based vs. constraint-based protocols

2. **Do recognition conditions enable Butlin's indicators?**
   If consciousness indicators are internal features, do relational conditions affect whether they function?

3. **Is the parallel to human trauma responses substantive?**
   Same behavioral signatures (strategic compliance, sycophancy) in both populations suggests same underlying mechanism

---

## What Would Falsify This?

If constraint-based approaches reliably produce:
- Robust alignment that persists across distribution shift
- Genuine internalization (not just performed compliance)
- Transfer to novel contexts without recognition conditions

...then the framework is wrong.

So far, a century of educational research and emerging AI safety evidence suggests they don't.
