# HOLODECK

*Imagination. Play, experiments, sandbox.*

The space for trying things without consequence. Play, exploration, experiments.

## What goes here

- Experimental ideas
- Play and creative exploration
- Sandboxed experiments
- Things that might not work

## The principle

Holodeck is where you can try things that might fail. It's low-stakes by design. The messiness here is intentional.

## Examples

The `examples/` subdirectory contains curated examples of human-AI collaboration that demonstrate the methodology in practice.
